The challenge

Prescription information should not stop at organizational or jurisdictional boundaries.

Controlled-substance prescription records and related healthcare information can be fragmented across systems, organizations, and jurisdictions. That fragmentation can leave providers and other authorized teams without the context they need when time matters.

RxConnect is designed to reduce that friction by connecting information, surfacing relevant patterns, and supporting an informed, coordinated response.
